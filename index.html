Proteine Recette Maison - Natural DIY Protein Hair Mask Recipe

🌾 Farine de riz 3 soto
🥛 Rooo 1 verre
🍯 Honey / Mofaiky 1 soto

Mode d’utilisation Step-by-Step:

  1. Andrahoana ambony alo malefaka (Boil gently on low heat until soft).
  2. Ahosotra taom-bolo maina (Apply evenly on dry hair).
  3. Pause 30 min (Leave on for 30 minutes).
  4. Sasana tsara (Rinse thoroughly).
  5. Araho leave-in creme na baume capillaire (Follow up with Tariq leave-in cream or balm while styling).
